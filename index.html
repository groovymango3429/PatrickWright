Primary Research

NASA Supercritical Water Oxidation (SCWO) System – Undergraduate Researcher

Creative Inquiry: Clemson University — Spherical Flames and Supercritical Oxidation: Applications in Aerospace Propulsion and Space Explorations

Dates: August 2026 - Present

Location: Clemson, SC

Recommissioning a NASA-developed SCWO apparatus by reverse-engineering control architecture and investigating interfaces across Leeds & Northrup LPU hardware, Allen-Bradley CompactLogix 5380 PLC systems, analog heater controls, I/O modules, instrumentation, and thermocouple safety circuits.

Work includes tracing electrical and communication interfaces from P&IDs, operations manuals, wiring diagrams, schematics, and physical hardware inspections, plus investigation of legacy PC-to-controller communication for restoration planning.
