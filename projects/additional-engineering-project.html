Science Olympiad Electric Vehicle

Role: Designer, Builder, and Programmer

Dates: October 2023 - June 2024

Designed, built, programmed, and tested a complete miniature electric vehicle for Science Olympiad competition.

Engineering Problem

The vehicle needed dependable control behavior and repeatable performance under competition constraints.

Approach

  • Built the mechanical structure and electrical system from the ground up.
  • Integrated motors, wiring, and ESP32-based control logic.
  • Ran iterative testing and tuning cycles to improve competition performance.

Results

The team won first place in a state competition.
