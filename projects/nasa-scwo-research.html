NASA Supercritical Water Oxidation (SCWO) System – Undergraduate Researcher

Role: Undergraduate Researcher

Creative Inquiry: Clemson University — Spherical Flames and Supercritical Oxidation: Applications in Aerospace Propulsion and Space Explorations

Dates: August 2026 - Present

Location: Clemson, SC

Recommissioning a NASA-developed SCWO system used for high-temperature, high-pressure waste-treatment research. The work focuses on restoring system monitoring, control, and data acquisition capability.

Engineering Problem

The platform includes mixed legacy and modern controls with incomplete interface records across power, instrumentation, and communication pathways.

Approach

  • Reverse-engineered control architecture spanning legacy and modern hardware.
  • Identified Leeds & Northrup LPU hardware, CompactLogix 5380 PLC hardware, analog heater controls, and I/O modules.
  • Mapped thermocouple safety circuits, solid-state relays, contactors, and 4-20 mA PLC interfaces.
  • Traced interfaces using P&IDs, operations manuals, wiring diagrams, schematics, and physical inspections.
  • Investigated legacy PC-to-controller communication and PLC connectivity pathways.

Current Status

Interface mapping and system understanding are being consolidated to support staged restoration of controls, monitoring, and data collection functions.
